# P1: gripped plate with a circular hole

Definition copied verbatim from the benchmark-pack specification (section 3.2).

Unless stated otherwise, use E = 1, ν = 0.3, plane stress and unit thickness.

**P1: gripped plate with a circular hole.**
- **Domain:** Ω = [−1,1]² minus the open disc of radius a = 1/2 centred at 0.
- **Grips:** frictionless grips at x = ±1, so u_x = ±δ/2 with δ = 1 and zero shear traction.
- **Free boundaries:** the edges y = ±1 and the hole are traction-free.
- **Symmetry:** a quarter model with symmetry planes x = 0 and y = 0 is allowed. With frictionless grips the vertical rigid translation is free; fix it with u_y = 0 on y = 0. This does not change K.
- **Quantities:**
  - Q1 = stiffness K = (total x-force on the grip at x = 1)/δ = a(u,u)/δ².
  - Q2 = the average of σ_xx over the patch P = {(r,θ): 1/2 ≤ r ≤ 0.55, |θ − π/2| ≤ 0.1}, in polar coordinates about the hole centre, i.e. (1/|P|) ∫_P σ_xx dA.
- **Optional variant P1c:** clamped grips, u = (±δ/2, 0).
- **Known answers for tests:** without the hole, K = 1 and Q2 = 0.5 exactly, because frictionless grips give a uniform uniaxial stress of 0.5.
