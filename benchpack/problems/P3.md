**P3: block with a spherical cavity (already built).**

  - body (−1,1)³, E = 1, ν = 3/10;
  - cavity of radius 1/2;
  - faces y, z = ±1 free;
  - grips at x = ±1 moved apart by δ, either clamped or frictionless.
- **Existing CERTIFIED values (N = 24):**

  - frictionless: 1.74510549 ≤ K ≤ 1.75827651.
- **Use:** include it as is, with its 0.8–0.9% width. Tighten it only if a partner asks for 3D.
