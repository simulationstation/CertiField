# P2: L-bracket

Definition copied verbatim from the benchmark-pack specification (section 3.2).

Unless stated otherwise, use E = 1, ν = 0.3, plane stress and unit thickness.

**P2: L-bracket.**
- **Domain:** Ω = (−1,1)² minus [0,1) × (−1,0], with the re-entrant corner at 0.
- **Clamped edge:** the left edge x = −1 is clamped (u = 0).
- **Moving grip:** the right edge x = 1 (0 ≤ y ≤ 1) is clamped to a rigid grip moved by u = (0, −δ), δ = 1.
- **Free boundaries:** all other edges are traction-free.
- **Quantities:**
  - Q1 = stiffness K = (total vertical force on the right grip)/δ = a(u,u)/δ².
  - Q2 = the average of σ_xx over the square [0, 0.1] × [0, 0.1].
- **Corner singularity:** stresses are singular at the corner, behaving like r^(−0.456), so uniform refinement converges slowly. Use refinement graded towards the corner, or singular enrichment. All integrals must remain rigorous; for non-polynomial enrichment, use interval Taylor-model quadrature with a proven remainder bound.
