## Before calling anything a defect

1. **NDSolve with its own mesh, P3: lands outside, above by 0.10 % (clamped) and 0.079 % (frictionless).**
   Polygonal geometry, not a solver defect. The meshed octant has volume 0.93518 against 0.93455 exactly
   (excess 6.27e-4, the cavity about 1 % too small) at every MaxCellMeasure from 1e-2 to 5e-5: ToElementMesh
   tessellates the sphere once and does not refine it with MaxCellMeasure; "MaxBoundaryCellMeasure" refines the
   surface mesh but leaves the new nodes on the old facets (volume excess 4.4e-4 at every setting tried). So the
   values converge to the stiffness of a body with a smaller, polyhedral cavity. The same solver on the gmsh meshes
   with the cavity on the sphere converges into the interval (row above). Grip conditions, quantity: as in the
   original script; K from the assembled system equals the script's NIntegrate value to 15 digits on these meshes.
   Locking: none (nu = 0.3, quadratic tetrahedra). The finest level (1e-5, about 750 000 unknowns) stops the kernel.
2. **NDSolve post-processing with NIntegrate (2D, and 3D on imported meshes): suspected defect in NIntegrate over
   ElementMesh regions (DIAGNOSTIC).** K computed as NIntegrate of the strain energy of the InterpolatingFunction
   differs from a(u_h, u_h) of the same solution, by up to about 1e-3 relative, and sometimes returns exactly 0
   (coarse curved meshes, P1 patch and P3). The solution itself is right: its energy from the assembled system
   equals scikit-fem's on the same mesh to 1e-12. Minimal reproducer `harness/repro_ndsolve_gradient.wls`, output
   `repro_ndsolve_gradient.txt`: the gradient of an ElementMeshInterpolation is the exact element gradient at every
   point tested, but NIntegrate of the piecewise constant |grad u|^2 over the ElementMesh region gives 0.999884
   instead of 1 for two triangles and is 0.30 % low for 158 triangles, unchanged with PrecisionGoal -> 10 or
   Method -> "FiniteElement" and without a message; integrating triangle by triangle gives the exact value.
   Ruled out: geometry (unit square, exact), boundary conditions (none), quantity (exact value known), locking (n/a).
   Effect on users: a stiffness from this post-processing can lie below the true K although the method gives an
   upper bound: P1, linear, 147 224 DOF: 0.5911386, below the certified lower end 0.5912009869 by 6.2e-5
   (1.1e-4 relative), while a(u_h, u_h) of the same solution is 0.5912075, above it.
3. **CalculiX plane-stress elements are 3D slabs.** ccx expands CPS3 / CPS6 into C3D6 / C3D15 elements of the
   section thickness. With thickness 1 the P1 stiffness at h = 0.2 is 0.6007 instead of 0.5962 (sigma_zz != 0): a
   3D slab, not plane stress. The harness uses thickness 1e-3 (K unchanged to 1e-6 relative down to 1e-4). With the
   expansion, CPS3 is stiffer than scikit-fem's linear triangles on the same mesh (it converges to the same limit);
   CPS6 and C3D10 give scikit-fem's values to 7 digits. Not a defect: documented behaviour; users must choose a thin
   section.
4. **CalculiX output has 7 significant digits** (.dat), so the 2e-9-wide P1 interval for K cannot be resolved from
   its output; the quadratic curved value agrees to those digits.
5. **Node order of 10-node tetrahedra in Mathematica** differs from VTK / Abaqus: mid-edge nodes (1,2) (2,3) (3,1)
   (2,4) (3,4) (1,4) (checked on ToElementMesh's own elements and on the volume of a unit tetrahedron). With the
   VTK order the kernel stopped without a message. The harness permutes the nodes (`run_ndsolve.py`).

## Upper bound on K, and where polygonal geometry breaks it

For these displacement-loaded problems a conforming displacement FE solution on a mesh that contains the body gives
K_h >= K. All harness meshes contain the body: the meshed hole or cavity boundary lies inside the circle or sphere
(`geometry_check.csv`: max(r - 1/2) <= 2e-16 for straight and for curved quadratic edges and faces; area or volume
excess positive). Accordingly every K value of scikit-fem, CalculiX and NDSolve (assembled system) is at or above the
certified lower end, and all P1 values are above the interval's lower end.
The bound can fail when the meshed hole is larger than the true one (nodes off the circle, a circumscribed polygon,
a CAD tessellation that cuts into the material), when a curved boundary is convex towards the material (a polygon
inscribed in a round outer edge removes material), with non-conforming or under-integrated elements, and with
post-processing that does not reproduce a(u_h, u_h) (finding 2). Isoparametric elements are integrated
numerically, so for them the bound holds only up to quadrature error.

## P1, straight-edged meshes: geometry against discretisation

`p1_geometry_split.csv` (`harness/p1_geometry_split.py`). K_h - K = discretisation + geometry, with K(Omega_h), the
stiffness of the polygonal plate of the mesh, from refinements of the same straight mesh (Richardson), and K the
midpoint of the certified interval. Quadratic elements, scikit-fem (CalculiX's CPS6 values agree to 7 digits):

| h (hole edges h/4) | DOF | K_h - K | discretisation | geometry | area excess of the quarter model |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.05 | 10 092 | 2.994e-5 | 3.6e-7 | 2.958e-5 | 2.03e-5 |
| 0.025 | 37 912 | 7.451e-6 | 3.5e-8 | 7.415e-6 | 5.09e-6 |
| 0.0125 | 148 160 | 1.860e-6 | 2.1e-9 | 1.858e-6 | 1.27e-6 |
| 0.00625 | 586 616 | 4.665e-7 | 1.2e-9 | 4.65e-7 (estimated*) | 3.19e-7 |

\* no refinement of this mesh fits the 4 GB cap; geometry = 1.458 x area excess, the factor fitted on the three
coarser levels (1.454, 1.458, 1.461).
So on straight-edged meshes the quadratic values sit above the interval almost entirely because of the polygonal hole
(99.7 % at the finest level); the geometry part falls like the square of the hole edge length, and the edges would
have to be about 19 times shorter than at the finest level (about 8e-5) for it alone to fall below the interval width
1.3e-9. Linear elements: at h = 0.00625 the excess 6.47e-6 is 6.01e-6 discretisation and 4.65e-7 geometry. For Q2 the
geometry part is below 1.5e-5 at every level (interval width 3.1e-5).
