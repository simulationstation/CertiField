- 2026-11-24. Audit references: screening uses Arb (python-flint) balls with at least 64 bits of relative accuracy, falling back to mpmath at 30 digits; every reported case is redone per spec 4.2 (mpmath at 50 and 100 digits agreeing to 20 digits, plus an Arb ball or, for inverse functions, an Arb bracket of the root). Reason: mpmath's built-in incomplete gamma and beta raise NoConvergence or stall for parameters above about 1e4, and Arb is much faster where it works. For those functions the mpmath reference is a continued fraction or power series in mpmath arithmetic (DLMF 8.7.1, 8.9.2, 8.17.22).
- 2026-11-24. Audit defect counting: a case counts when it is a wrong special value (NaN, inf, 0 or wrong sign where a finite non-zero value is correct), or when its error exceeds the spec 4.4 threshold and also exceeds 16 kappa u (kappa the condition number with respect to the inputs, u = 2^-53 or 2^-24). Cases within 16 kappa u (near zeros of digamma, oscillating Bessel functions) are listed as conditioning-limited and not counted. Reason: such errors are what an ulp-sized change of the input produces, and maintainers treat them as a different class.

- 2026-11-25. Audit: results whose exact value lies below the smallest normal number (subnormal range or below), returned as 0 or clamped to the smallest normal number, are listed as "underflow handling" (low severity) and not counted as defects. Reason: relative accuracy in the subnormal range is not achievable in general and none of the libraries documents it; the cases are still listed in the summary.
- 2026-11-25. Reason: a deliberate safety margin of three decades above DBL_MIN, with no practical effect.
